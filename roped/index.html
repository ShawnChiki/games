Roped

Two climbers, two gravities, one rope. Climb through the painting together. If your partner falls, only a grip on a fence or a line will hold them.

  • Left thumb moves. Drag on the right to look around.
  • Hold Grab near a chain fence or a striped line to hang on and climb.
  • Blocks give footing. Glossy shapes are slippery. Fences can only be climbed.
  • Reel in only takes up slack. Pay out gives your partner more rope.
