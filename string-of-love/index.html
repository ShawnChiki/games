String of Love

Shan the climbing architect has to lasso Eliza, a bouncy gummy princess in a mushroom hat, and walk her home to the elves. Eliza would rather float him up to the aliens.

Turn your phone sideways for the best view.

On your own, against a bot

With a friend on another phone

Shan

  • Left thumb walks. Walk into a wall or plank to climb it.
  • Tap Eliza to throw your lasso. If she holds still, you get her.
  • Next to a wall or plank, hold Hold on to grip it. Your grip runs out.
  • Hold Pull in to reel her down. Walk her into a glowing ring.
  • You carry the only berries. Toss them to Eliza; elves at the cottages give you more.
  • Only Eliza's kisses keep your love up. Run out and you nap on your phone.

Eliza

  • Left thumb rolls you around. Tap to hop. Hold Bounce for a big one, tap again in the air for a double bounce.
  • Puff up to float. Anvil feet make you heavy. Flip for tricks.
  • Wind blows the way you are steering. Mushroom pads launch you sky high.
  • Chew ropes. Only Shan has berries, and berries refill your magic.
  • Kiss Shan to keep him going. If he naps, he is easy to float away.
  • Explode into a swarm of mini Elizas to break free and smother Shan in kisses.
  • Lift Shan past the sky line to win.

The camera stays put unless you move it. Drag on the right side to turn and tilt it; scroll to zoom. The arrow buttons at the top turn it too. Keyboard: WASD move, Space jump, Q let go, E berry, F tie, R pull, H hold on, click to throw. As Eliza: Space bounce, Shift puff, X anvil, V flip, Q wind, E explode, C chew.
