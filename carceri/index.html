How to play

  1. Study the plan. Every run is a new prison. The plan in your cell shows where you are and the way out. The lockdown clock is already running while you look.
  2. Get up and out. Climb, jump, balance and wall-run up to a doorway of daylight before the gates come down.
  3. Lost? Every cell has the plan on its wall. Fall too far and you wake up in a new cell with a new number.
